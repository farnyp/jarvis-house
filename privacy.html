Privacy policy

Jarvis House is a personal assistant operated by Francis Lee. Contact: jarvishermesinbox@gmail.com.

Google information accessed

With account-specific OAuth consent, the assistant may access Gmail messages and labels, calendars and events, Drive files and metadata, and Docs and Sheets contents. Access supports organizing mail and files, planning, and creating or editing content at the owner's direction. Sending email and externally sharing content require approval.

Storage and processing

OAuth credentials are stored locally, separately from these public pages. Relevant records and outputs may be retained on the owner's computer, in authorized Google accounts, in backups if configured, and in Hermes conversation history. Requested AI tasks may send relevant content to configured AI and tool providers, including Nous and configured model providers. Messages sent through Discord are processed by Discord. The system is not represented as entirely local or end-to-end encrypted.

Use and disclosure

Google user data is used to provide the owner's requested assistant features. It is not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally supplied to train generalized AI models. The operator does not authorize independent provider reuse for model training. Only necessary information should be sent to service providers. Jarvis House's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

Records are retained for ongoing tasks and history until removed under the owner's retention choices or applicable provider requirements. No fixed automatic deletion period is configured. The owner can revoke API access through Google Account connections and request removal of local credentials and records. Revocation does not automatically delete previously retained files, backups or conversation history; those copies require separate removal, including from relevant providers.

Public website

These pages contain no connected-account records, credentials, login form or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages may process request metadata under GitHub's privacy policy. Private records must not be published in this repository.
