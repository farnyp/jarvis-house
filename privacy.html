Privacy policy

Jarvis House is a personal assistant system operated by Francis Lee for his own connected accounts. Contact: jarvishermesinbox@gmail.coma>.

Google information accessed

With account-specific OAuth consent, the assistant may access Gmail messages and labels, calendars and events, Drive files and metadata, and Docs and Sheets contents. Requested access supports organizing mail and files, planning, and creating or editing documents and spreadsheets at the owner's direction. It may include permission to send email; sending, external sharing and other consequential actions require the owner's approval.

Storage and processing

OAuth tokens are stored locally in separate account credential directories, outside this public website and repository. Relevant records and task outputs may be retained on the owner's computer, in private backups, in the authorized Google accounts, and in Hermes conversation history. When requested tasks require AI reasoning or tools, relevant content may be processed by the configured AI and tool providers, currently including Nous and its configured model providers. Information sent to Discord is also processed by Discord. The system is not represented as entirely local or end-to-end encrypted.

Use and disclosure

Google user data is used only to provide the owner's requested assistant features. It is not sold, used for advertising, or intentionally supplied to train generalized AI models. The operator does not authorize independent provider reuse of Google data for model training. Only necessary information should be sent to service providers. Jarvis House's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

Records are retained for the owner's ongoing tasks
