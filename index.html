Jarvis House

A personal assistant system operated by Francis Lee using Hermes Agent. It helps its owner manage email, calendars, files, documents and spreadsheets through explicitly authorized Google APIs.

This public website contains application information only. It does not provide a public login or display connected-account records or credentials.

The assistant is for personal use, not a service offered to the public. Each connected Google account requires its owner's consent.
